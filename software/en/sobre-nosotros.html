About us

curious people researching the flora of Urabá

From Urabá, with curiosity and as a team

At Plantas de Urabá we are curious people who research the plants of the region. We do not sell seedlings: we document what we learn about the flora of the Antioquia humid tropics and share it openly.

The register already brings together 100 species among agricultural plants, palms, timber trees and native flora, and it grows alongside what we keep discovering together with the community of Urabá.

  • Open profiles with scientific names, uses and real photos.
  • Ongoing research on crops, shade systems and conservation.
  • Commitment to making the tropical rainforest visible and protecting it.

Our region

Urabá is a subregion of the department of Antioquia, in northwestern Colombia, washed by the Gulf of Urabá and crossed by rivers such as the Atrato. Its warm, humid climate makes it one of the country's most biodiverse and agriculturally promising areas.

From here come export banana, the hartón plantain of the Colombian table, aroma cacao, oil palm and forest treasures such as borojó, achiote and bijao.

Every profile we share is a piece of the humid tropics inviting us to keep researching.
